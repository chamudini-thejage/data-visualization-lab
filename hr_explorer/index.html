Lines of constant radius

On log axes the Stefan–Boltzmann law becomes straight lines: log L = 2 log R + 4 log(T/5778). Stars on the same line share a radius, so white dwarfs sit on the 0.01 R☉ line and hypergiants beyond 1000 R☉.

Two axes, one classifier

Temperature alone cannot tell supergiants from dwarfs, and brightness alone cannot tell white dwarfs from red dwarfs. Together they separate all six types; in the lab a decision tree on these two axes classified 99.6 % of stars correctly.

How the prediction works

Your star’s log temperature, log luminosity and log radius are standardised and compared with all 240 stars. The five closest stars vote; the bars show the vote. Example stars use approximate published values.
